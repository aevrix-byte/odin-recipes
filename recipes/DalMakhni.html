Dal Makhni

DAAL MAKHNI - THE REAL TASTE OF NORTH

Dal Makhni is a rich and creamy North Indian dish made with black lentils (urad dal) and kidney beans (rajma), slow-cooked with butter, tomatoes, and aromatic spices. It is traditionally served with naan, roti, or rice.

INGREDIENTS

    For about 4–5 servings:
  • 1 cup whole black urad dal (sabut urad)
  • ¼ cup rajma (kidney beans)
  • 3–4 tbsp butter (plus extra for serving)
  • 1 tbsp oil or ghee
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 2 medium tomatoes, puréed
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1–2 green chilies, finely chopped
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • ½–1 tsp kasuri methi
  • ½ cup fresh cream (optional, but gives it a richer texture)
  • Salt, to taste
  • 4–5 cups water, as needed
  • Fresh coriander, for garnish

STEPS TO PREPARE IT

  1. Soak the lentils:

    Wash the urad dal and rajma and soak them in plenty of water for 8 hours or overnight.

  2. Cook the dal:

    Drain and rinse. Add the dal and rajma to a pressure cooker with about 4 cups of water and some salt. Cook until very soft. Mash slightly with the back of a spoon

  3. Prepare the masala:

    Heat oil and 1 tbsp butter in a pan. Add onion and sauté until golden. Add ginger-garlic paste and green chilies and cook for 1–2 minutes.

  4. Add tomatoes and spices:

    Add tomato purée, turmeric, chili powder, coriander powder, and cumin powder. Cook until the oil begins to separate from the masala.

  5. Combine:

    Add the cooked dal and rajma. Mix well and add water as needed to achieve a creamy consistency.

  6. Slow-cook:

    Simmer on low heat for 30–45 minutes, stirring occasionally. Add the remaining butter while cooking.

  7. Finish:

    Add garam masala, kasuri methi, and cream. Simmer for another 5 minutes.

  8. Serve:

    Garnish with fresh coriander and a little butter or cream. Serve hot with naan, roti, or basmati rice.
