LASAGNA


LASAGNA A DELICIOUS DISH

Lasagna, also known by the plural form lasagne, is a type of pasta made in wide, flat sheets. It originates in Italian cuisine, where it is served in a number of ways, but is most often served as a casserole made by stacked layers of pasta, alternating with fillings that could consist of meat, dairy, vegetables, and sauces. Typically, cooked pasta is assembled with the aforementioned fillings, topped with grated cheese, and then baked in an oven (al forno). Regional variations of this are found across Italy, along with Americanized versions worldwide.

INGREDIENTS

      🧀 For the Lasagna
  • Lasagna sheets -> 912 sheets
  • Mozzarella cheese -> 2 cups, grated
  • Parmesan cheese -> ½ cup, grated
  • Olive oil -> 2 tbsp

STEPS TO PREPARE IT

  1. Make the meat sauce:

    Brown ground beef or Italian sausage with diced onion and garlic. Drain excess fat, then add tomato sauce/crushed tomatoes, a little tomato paste, salt, pepper, and Italian herbs. Simmer for about 20–30 minutes.

  2. Prepare the noodles:

    Boil lasagna noodles according to the package directions until just tender, then drain. If using oven-ready noodles, you can skip this step.

  3. Prepare the cheese filling:

    Mix ricotta with an egg, grated Parmesan, a little salt and pepper, and optionally chopped parsley. Have shredded mozzarella ready separately.

  4. Layer the lasagna:

    Spread a thin layer of meat sauce over the bottom of a baking dish. Add noodles, ricotta mixture, mozzarella, and more meat sauce. Repeat the layers until the dish is filled, usually about 3 layers.

  5. Finish the top:

    Cover the final noodle layer with sauce and a generous amount of mozzarella and Parmesan.

  6. Bake:

    Cover with foil and bake at 375°F (190°C) for about 25 minutes. Remove the foil and bake another 15–20 minutes, until bubbling and browned on top.

  7. Let it rest:

    This is important—leave the lasagna for 10–15 minutes before cutting so the layers set and don't slide apart.

A typical 9×13-inch lasagna uses roughly 12 noodles, 1–1½ lb meat, 24–32 oz tomato sauce, 15 oz ricotta, 2–3 cups mozzarella, and ½–1 cup Parmesan.
