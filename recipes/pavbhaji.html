PAV BHAJI - A SUPER TASTY NORTH INDIAN DISH

DESCRIPTION

Pav Bhaji is a flavorsome and hearty meal that has a delicious blend of spicy mixed vegetables, served alongside soft butter toasted dinner rolls, crunchy onions and lemon wedges. This homemade Mumbai street style snack meal tastes absolutely phenomenal and is a total crowd-pleaser with bold flavors. It’s the easiest and a great dinner option you can make on busy weeknights. Make the BEST Pav Bhaji at home with my simple recipe instructions to make it in a regular pan or stovetop pressure cooker or Instant pot.

INGREDIENTS

    For the bhaji:
  • 3 medium potatoes, boiled and mashed
  • 1 cup cauliflower, chopped
  • ½ cup green peas
  • 1 capsicum, finely chopped
  • 2 medium tomatoes, finely chopped
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 1–2 green chilies, chopped
  • 1½ tbsp pav bhaji masala
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2–3 tbsp butter
  • 1 cup water
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • Fresh coriander, chopped
    For the pav:
  • 6–8 pav buns
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • A little pav bhaji masala
  • Chopped coriander, optional

STEPS TO PREPARE IT

  1. Cook the vegetables:

    : Boil the potatoes, cauliflower and peas until soft. Drain and mash them together.

  2. Make the masala:

    Heat butter in a large pan. Add onions and sauté until translucent. Add ginger-garlic paste and green chilies; cook for 1 minute.

  3. Add vegetables:

    Add capsicum and cook for 2–3 minutes. Add tomatoes, turmeric, chili powder and pav bhaji masala. Cook until the tomatoes become soft and the butter starts separating.

  4. Combine:

    Add the mashed vegetables, salt and about 1 cup water. Mash everything together with a potato masher while simmering.

  5. Simmer:

    Cook for 8–10 minutes, stirring and mashing occasionally. Add more water if you want a looser, smoother bhaji.

  6. Finish:

    Stir in lemon juice and fresh coriander. Add a little extra butter for a richer taste.

  7. Toast the pav:

    Slice the pav horizontally. Heat butter on a pan, sprinkle a little pav bhaji masala, and toast the pav on both sides until golden.

  8. Serve:

    Serve the hot bhaji with buttered pav, chopped onion, coriander and lemon wedges.

Tip:For that classic street-style texture, mash the bhaji really well while it simmers and don't skimp on the butter.
